About Celtic Cup Coffeehouse

Denise and Chris Smith run the Celtic Cup. It is in a revamped 1928 Craftsman-style house with Irish and Scottish touches. The upstairs Gathering Room can be reserved for a party, reception or meeting.

  • Full barista service
  • British afternoon teas
  • Gathering Room to reserve
