    The Upstate grime calendar

    What builds up on a house and a car here, month by month, and the job that deals with it.

    What builds up
    JanFebMarAprMayJunJulAugSepOctNovDec
    Pollen film
    Mar to May

    Spring pollen coats cars, porches and screens in a yellow-green film. Book the wash after the heaviest weeks, or it settles right back. Car detailing

    Mildew in the shade
    May to Sep

    Warm, humid months grow green and black mildew on north walls, shaded decks and fences. Power washing

    Red clay splash
    Jun to Aug

    Summer downpours splash red clay up the bottom of siding and garage doors. It stains if it sits. Power washing

    Leaf stains
    Oct to Dec

    Wet leaves left on concrete and decks leave brown marks that get harder to lift each week. Power washing

    Garage clear-out
    Dec to Feb (over the new year)

    Boxes pile up over the holidays. Late winter is a calm time to clear the garage before spring projects. Junk removal
