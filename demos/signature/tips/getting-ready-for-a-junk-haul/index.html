Getting ready for a junk haul

Sort, measure and clear a path before the truck comes, and the haul goes faster and costs less. A checklist for the day before.

Oct 2026, 1 min read. By the Upstate Clean Co. crew.

A junk haul is usually priced by how much space your things take in the truck. A little preparation makes it faster and keeps the load smaller.

Sort first

Walk the space with three kinds of sticky notes or tape: keep, donate and haul. Anything not marked stays. It takes an hour and stops the crew from asking about every box.

Know what most haulers cannot take

Many haulers cannot take paint, oil, pool chemicals, propane tanks or batteries, because they need special disposal. Ask before the day, and set those items aside. Your county's household waste drop-off usually takes them.

Measure the tight spots

If a sofa or a freezer came in through a narrow door, it has to go out the same way. Note the width of doors and stairways and mention big items when you ask for the price.

Clear a path

  • Make room for the truck as close to the door as you can.
  • Keep a clear walkway from the pile to the door.
  • Put pets in a closed room for the visit.

Donations instead of the landfill

Furniture and appliances in working order can often go to a local charity instead of the landfill. Mention them when you book, so they can be loaded last and dropped off first.

Take a photo

A wide photo of the pile, with something for scale like a door or a car, gives the crew most of what they need for a price before they arrive.
