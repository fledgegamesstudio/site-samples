Keeping a car clean through pollen season

Spring pollen scratches paint when you wipe it dry. How to rinse it off safely and keep the inside of the car breathable.

Nov 2026, 1 min read. By the Upstate Clean Co. crew.

Spring pollen looks soft, but the grains are gritty. Wiping it off a dry car drags them across the paint and leaves fine scratches you see in the sun.

Rinse, never wipe dry

Rinse the car with plenty of water first, so the pollen floats off. Then wash with a clean mitt and car shampoo, working from the roof down. Use a second bucket of plain water to rinse the mitt.

Do the wheels first

Wheels hold the dirtiest grit. Clean them with their own brush before you touch the paint, so that grit never gets near the body.

Keep the inside breathable

  • Run the air on recirculate on the worst pollen days.
  • Vacuum the floor mats and the vents, where pollen collects.
  • Ask about the cabin air filter at your next service; a clogged one makes the car feel stuffy.

Protect the paint

A coat of wax or sealant before spring makes pollen and sap rinse off more easily. Park away from trees that drip sap if you can.

When to book a detail

The best time for a full detail is after the heavy pollen weeks are over, when the inside and the outside can be cleaned once and stay that way.
