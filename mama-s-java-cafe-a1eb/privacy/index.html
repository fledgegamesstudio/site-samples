Privacy

What Mama's Java Cafe collects when you use this website, why, and how to have it deleted. Updated November 24, 2026.

What we collect

  • When you ask for a quote: your name, phone, email, where the job is, your message and any photos you add.
  • Visits: we count page visits on our own server to see which pages help people. There are no advertising trackers on this site.

Why

To answer you, schedule and do the work, send confirmations and reminders, and keep a record of the job. We never sell your information or share it for anyone else's advertising.

Who else handles it

  • Our web host, which stores this site.
  • Our email provider, which sends and receives our email.

Keeping it and deleting it

To have your information deleted, email us at the address below. We keep a record that past jobs happened (date, service, price) without your contact details, for our business records.

Children

This site and app are for adults booking work. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Questions

Email mamasjavatn@yahoo.com or call (615) 776-5282. If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
