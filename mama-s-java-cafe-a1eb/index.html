Questions people ask

What is flavored coffee?

Flavored coffee is regular coffee with a flavor such as pecan, toffee or berry added to the beans before brewing. The cup smells and tastes of that flavor, and it is usually served the same way as any other coffee.

How does an air roaster roast coffee beans?

An air roaster keeps the beans moving in a stream of hot air instead of a spinning drum. The beans roast evenly as they float, and the person roasting watches their color until they reach the roast level they want.

What is in a pastry?

Most pastries are made from flour, butter, sugar and eggs. Some use a flaky layered dough and others a soft sweet dough, and they are often filled or topped with fruit, nuts or icing.

How long should tea steep?

It depends on the kind. Black tea needs a few minutes in hot water, green tea needs less and cooler water, and herbal blends often steep a little longer. Steeping too long makes tea bitter.
