Tea in Nolensville

Leaves steeped in hot water, served black, green or herbal.

Tea is made by steeping dried leaves or herbs in hot water. Black tea is strong and dark, green tea is lighter and grassy, and herbal blends use flowers, fruit or spices instead of tea leaves.

A cup of tea can be taken plain or with milk, lemon or honey. It has less caffeine than coffee, which makes it a common choice later in the day or for anyone who wants a gentler drink.

What it usually has

  • Black, green and herbal types
  • Leaves steeped in hot water
  • Milk, lemon or honey on the side
  • Lower in caffeine than coffee
