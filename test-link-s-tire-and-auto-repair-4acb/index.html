Questions people ask

How often should tires be rotated?

Many car makers suggest a rotation about every six months or whenever the oil is changed. Moving the tires around helps them wear evenly, so they last longer and grip the road better.

How can I tell when a tire needs replacing?

Look for low tread, cracks in the sidewall, bulges, or a nail or cut that has caused a slow leak. Uneven wear or a steady vibration while driving can also mean a tire is past its useful life.

What does a warning light on the dash mean?

A warning light tells you a system in the car has found a fault. A diagnostic scanner reads the stored code, which points the mechanic toward the cause so the right part gets checked first.

What are the signs of worn brakes?

Squealing or grinding when you stop, a soft pedal, or the car pulling to one side are common signs. Brake pads wear down over time, and checking them early can protect the rotors from damage.
