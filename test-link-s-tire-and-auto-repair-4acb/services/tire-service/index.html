Tire service in Lynchburg

Fitting, checking and swapping the tires on a car or truck.

Tire service covers the work that keeps the tires on a car in good shape. That includes taking a tire off the wheel, fitting a new one, and putting the wheel back on the car so it sits true.

Tires wear down with use, and nails, curbs and rough roads can damage them. A shop checks the tread and the sidewalls, looks for leaks, and sets the air to the level the car maker lists.

What is included

  • Mounting and balancing tires
  • Tire rotation
  • Flat tire repair
  • Checking tread and air pressure
