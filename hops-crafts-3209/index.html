About Hops & Crafts

Hops & Crafts is a craft beer taproom and bar in The Gulch, Nashville. We opened on 12th Ave on July 4, 2013. You can get pints, growler fills and shareable pub fare.

  • 36 rotating taps
  • Trivia night on Mondays
  • Half-price pub grub on Thursdays
