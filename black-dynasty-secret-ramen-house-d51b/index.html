About Black Dynasty Secret Ramen House

Black Dynasty is a ramen restaurant in Nashville. Everything is made from scratch, including the noodles, broth and toppings. The team ran the ramen counter at the Bearded Iris Sylvan Supply taproom and is planning its first standalone restaurant in East Nashville.
