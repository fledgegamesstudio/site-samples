About Historic Perk

We serve coffee, breakfast and lunch, and brunch on Saturdays. We roast our own single-origin coffee in the back of the shop. Syrups are made from scratch.

  • Roasted in the shop
  • Syrups made from scratch
  • Mobile coffee cart
