About FP Staging and Design

Cory and Anthea Click started the company in 2007. They stage luxury homes from a 14,000 square-foot warehouse of furnishings and decor in Nashville. Over 3,000 Nashville area homes have been staged and sold.

  • Over 3,000 homes staged and sold
  • 14,000 square-foot warehouse
  • Started in 2007

Services

  • Staging

    In-home evaluation, a custom plan, one-day installation and quick removal when the home sells.

  • Same Day Staging and Photography

    Most properties can be photographed the same day they are staged.

  • Consultations

    For DIY homeowners, with up to two hours with the founder and lead designer.

  • Model Home Design

    For residential builders and apartment developers.

  • E-Design Services

    Offered in three packages: Silver, Gold and Platinum.

  • FP Home Design

    The team plans and carries out the transformation of a client's home.

  • Price-Appropriate Furnishings

    Furniture quality is matched to the home's price level and style.
