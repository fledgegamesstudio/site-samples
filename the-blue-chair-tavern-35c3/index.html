About The Blue Chair Tavern

Jimmy and Sarah Wilson bought the building in 2012 and created the Tavern, with a staircase down to the Blue Chair Cafe. The tavern side was built around 1908 and housed the railroad Express office.

  • Jackalope Brewery craft beer
  • Spoken Word and Trivia events
  • Football on the TVs
