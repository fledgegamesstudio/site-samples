About Paris Winery & Vineyards

Lisa and Ruggero Ciarrocchi opened the winery in 2008. The vineyard has nearly 10,000 vines and grows Concord and Muscadine grapes. The grounds include a tasting room and a restaurant, and the winery is on the Wine Trail of West Tennessee.
