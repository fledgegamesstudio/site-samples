About Murfreesboro Art League

We are a nonprofit founded by local artists in 1966 to build and sustain an active arts community in Murfreesboro. The league hosts the Cannonsburgh gallery, organizes city art exhibitions, and teaches classes.

  • Founded in 1966
  • Gallery is free to view
  • Classes and city exhibits

What we do

  • Oil painting classes

    Adult classes or open studio, first three Monday nights of each month, 7 to 9 p.m.

  • Acrylic painting classes

  • Watercolor classes

  • Scratch board classes

  • Encaustics classes

  • Batik classes

  • Cannonsburgh gallery

    Free to view.

  • City Hall Rotunda exhibits

    Members' works are shown there.
