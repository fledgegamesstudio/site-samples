About Lucky Thai

Lucky Thai serves Laotian and Thai food. The menu has vegan, vegetarian, dairy-free and gluten-free options. It is run by owner Thip Phommavong, her daughter, and son-in-law. You can order take-out or delivery. Say vegan when you order.
