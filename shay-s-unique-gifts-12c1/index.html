About Shay's Unique Gifts

Shay's Unique Gifts is a gift shop on Commerce Street in Lynchburg. Shay Borel owns it and moved to town in the 1980s. The shop is known for handmade and repurposed items.

  • Handmade and repurposed items
  • Open for almost 20 years
  • Behind the red double doors

In the shop

  • Hand-painted furniture

  • Lamps

    Made from used wine and liquor bottles.

  • Coin jewelry

    Made from coins collected on the owner's travels.

  • Boots

    From Turkey and Mexico.

  • Leather bags and wallets

    Also wristlets and handbags.

  • Glassware and stemware

  • Christmas ornaments

  • Jewelry and clothing

    Also trinkets and accessories.
