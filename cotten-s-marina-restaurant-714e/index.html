About Cotten's Marina & Restaurant

Cotten's is a small full-service marina on the Caney Fork River in Rock Island. Greg and Sherry Cotten own it. The Island Grille restaurant overlooks the river.

  • Covered boat slips
  • Restaurant with bar
  • Dry storage
