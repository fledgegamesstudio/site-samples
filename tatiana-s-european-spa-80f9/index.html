Questions people ask

What is a buccal massage?

A buccal massage is a facial massage where the therapist works the muscles of the cheeks and jaw, sometimes with a gloved hand inside the mouth. It aims to ease tension in the face.

What is lymphatic drainage?

Lymphatic drainage is a very light, slow massage that follows the paths of the lymph vessels. It is meant to encourage the body to move fluid along and can leave the area feeling less puffy.

What is a sound bath?

A sound bath is a quiet session where you lie down while singing bowls, gongs or chimes are played. The slow tones are meant to help the body and mind settle into a relaxed state.

How often should a person get a facial?

Skin renews itself over a few weeks, so many skin care guides suggest a facial every few weeks. People with sensitive skin may space them further apart and let the skin rest between visits.

How should skin be cared for after waxing?

Skin is often tender after waxing, so it helps to avoid hot baths, hard exercise and tight clothing for the rest of the day. A gentle moisturizer can calm the area.
