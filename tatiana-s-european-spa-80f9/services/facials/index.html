Facials in Franklin

A skin care treatment that cleanses, exfoliates and moisturizes the face.

A facial is a series of steps for the skin of the face and neck. It usually begins with cleansing, then gentle exfoliation to lift away dead skin cells, followed by a mask and a moisturizer to finish.

The steps change with skin type. Dry skin often gets richer creams, oily skin gets lighter ones, and sensitive skin gets gentle products. A facial can include a short face and shoulder massage to help the skin and muscles relax.

What it usually includes

  • Cleansing
  • Gentle exfoliation
  • Mask
  • Moisturizer
  • Face and neck massage
