Questions people ask

How much water does a new lawn need?

A new lawn needs the top layer of soil kept moist until the roots take hold. After that, deeper and less frequent watering builds stronger roots than a light sprinkle every day.

What is the difference between drip and spray irrigation?

Spray heads cover open lawn by throwing water over an area. Drip lines place water slowly at the base of plants, which suits beds, shrubs and trees and loses less to wind and evaporation.

How can I tell if my yard has a drainage problem?

Look for water that pools after rain, soggy or mossy ground, washed out soil, and damp spots near the foundation. Problems that repeat after every storm usually need a drain or a change in slope.

Why does grading matter before building a patio?

A patio needs a firm, level base that also sheds water. Grading sets the slope so rain runs away from the house, and compacting the soil keeps pavers from sinking or shifting over time.
