Excavation and grading in Franklin

Digging, shaping and leveling ground before building or planting.

Excavation is the digging and moving of soil to make room for a patio, wall, pipe or planting bed. Grading shapes the ground so it has the right height and slope for its use.

Site leveling prepares bare ground before building or seeding. A level, compacted base helps pavers stay flat and helps water run off in the right direction instead of toward the house.

What is included

  • Digging for beds, trenches and bases
  • Grading to set slope and height
  • Leveling and compacting soil
  • Clearing ground for new work
