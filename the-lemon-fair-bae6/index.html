About The Lemon Fair

Gay Alvarez and Marilyn Powell opened The Lemon Fair in 1972 as a needlework shop on Georgia Avenue. It grew into a gallery and gift shop. Andrea, Scott and Dan bought the store in 2021.

  • A Sewanee gift stop since 1972
  • Work by local artists
  • Locally made pottery
