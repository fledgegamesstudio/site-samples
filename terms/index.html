These terms are the agreement between you (the business owner who signs up) and Evan Miller, who does business as Evan Miller Websites ("we", "us"). You accept them when you tick the box and pay on our checkout page. Please read them; they are short on purpose.

1. What you get

For as long as your plan is active, we:

  • build and publish a website for your business;
  • host it, keep it online and keep its software up to date;
  • make small changes you send us by email or text, such as new hours, prices, photos, menu items or wording;
  • set up a contact or quote form that sends messages to you.

A new page costs about $25 each. Anything bigger, such as a redesign, online booking or a store, gets a price by email first. We never start paid extra work without your OK.

We aim to make small changes within 2 business days.

2. Price and billing

  • The plan is $25 a month, or $250 a year paid up front.
  • Your first month is free. You enter your card when you sign up, and the first charge comes 30 days later. If you cancel before then, you pay nothing.
  • After that, your card is charged automatically every month (or every year on the yearly plan). Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.
  • There is no setup fee.

3. Your price stays the same

The price you sign up at is your price for as long as you stay with us, even if we charge new customers more later.

The one exception: if our own costs to keep your site running rise so much that we would make less than 5% on your plan, we may raise your price only as much as needed to get back to that 5%. We will tell you by email at least 30 days before any change, and you can cancel before it takes effect.

4. Cancelling

  • You can cancel any time by email or text. There is no minimum term and no cancellation fee.
  • Your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and then it stops. We do not refund part of a month or year already paid.
  • When your plan ends, we take the site offline, and on request we send you a copy of your site's files and everything you gave us, so you can host it anywhere you like.

5. What you own

  • Your domain name (for example, yourbusiness.com) is registered in your name and paid for by you. It is always yours. If you do not have one yet, we will walk you through getting one.
  • Your business name, logo, photos, text and other material you give us stay yours.
  • The site's design and code are ours, and we let you use them for your business for as long as your plan is active. When you leave, you get a copy of your site's files to keep using.

6. Your part

  • You tell us accurate information about your business (hours, prices, services, contact details), and you let us know when something changes.
  • You only send us text, photos, logos and other material that you own or have permission to use.
  • You do not ask us to put anything on your site that is illegal, misleading or harmful to others.

We may decline a change that would break these rules, and we will tell you why.
