About Nashville Gun Club

We are home grounds of the Tennessee State Trapshooting Association. We host sanctioned ATA trap shoots, regional competitions and league events. We also host charity sporting clays events.

  • Three sporting clays courses
  • Shotgun rentals
  • Handicap accessible
