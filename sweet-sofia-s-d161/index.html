About Sweet Sofia's

Sweet Sofia's is a Mexican ice cream shop on Old Harding Pike in Bellevue. It has a large selection of paletas. The shop is run by a mom and daughter.

  • Large paleta selection
  • Nondairy options
  • Run by mom and daughter

What we carry

  • Paletas

    Mexican popsicles, with many nondairy options.

  • Mangonada

    Ice cream with chamoy and fresh mangos.

  • Crepes

  • Churros

  • Fresas con crema

  • House-made flan

  • Ice cream

  • Smoothies
